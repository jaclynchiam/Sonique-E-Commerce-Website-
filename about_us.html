ABOUT

SONIQUE

Welcome to Sonique — where sound meets unique soul. We’re not just a headphone brand; We are here to make your music sound better, feel better, and just more you.

At Sonique, we believe music isn’t just something you hear but something you feel and that’s why we design headphones with crisp sound, deep bass, and comfy fit, so every beat hits just right for YOU.

Inspired by sounds and styles, Sonique brings you high performance and quality of audio, minimalist design. Whether you’re on the move, chilling at home, or deep in your favourite tracks, Sonique fits seamlessly into your everyday lifestyles.

About Our Founders

Dashvin Kumar - Web Development

For this project, I was responsible for the web development portion of the website. I developed the About Us page, FAQ, Login, Sign-Up, Reset Password, Forgot Password, Contact Us and Review pages. I also programmed all the Javascript components throughout the entire website.

This included implementing comprehensive form input and validation, interactive FAQ toggling, standard user feedback to enhance the user experience.

This project was a valuable opportunity for me to apply what I’ve learned in Web Fundamentals, sharpen my front-end development skills and better understand how different technologies work together to create a functional, interactive website.

Jaclyn Chiam - Web Development

I was responsible for the overall layout and visual design of the website. I began by creating a detailed Figma prototype that helped our team visualize the structure, user flow, and aesthetics of the site before development started. From there, I translated the design into code using HTML and CSS, ensuring the pages were clean, responsive, and aligned with modern web design standards.

Working on this project allowed me to deepen my understanding of front-end development and improve the way I bring designs to life through code. It was a rewarding experience to see the interface evolve from concept to a functional and polished website.

Olivia Goh - Marketing & Content

I was responsible for the marketing strategy and content creation for this project. I crafted clear, engaging content for the website, including the Frequently Asked Questions and About Us pages, ensuring our messaging was concise and informative.

I also coordinated documentation and helped highlight the project’s features to showcase its strengths and usability. This experience strengthened my communication skills and showed me how important content is for user engagement and website success.

Sandra - Marketing & Content

I contributed to the marketing and content aspects of this project by helping to develop clear, user-friendly copy for the website. I assisted in crafting the FAQs and About Us sections to ensure our messages were engaging and easy to understand.

Working alongside the team, I helped make sure the content supported the overall goals of the project and enhanced the user experience.
